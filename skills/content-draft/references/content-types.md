# Content types

Each type has a job, required parts and a minimum length. Briefs pick one; drafts follow it; QA checks the minimums. Add company-specific types in the workspace's `context/`, not here.

## Customer story
**Job:** proof for the buyer who has to justify the purchase.
**Required:** challenge in the customer's terms · what they tried before · what was implemented (products by name) · a numeric result · a named quote with title · links to the matching product or integration pages
**Minimum:** 600 words
**Notes:** no number, no story. Get the result approved by the customer before publishing.

## Integration / partner page
**Job:** answer "does this work with my system, and how?" for a buyer comparing options.
**Required:** what data or actions flow each way · 2–3 use cases · setup type and typical time · customer proof (link a story) · docs link · partner description in your words, not the partner's tagline
**Minimum:** 400 words of unique copy (template text doesn't count)
**Notes:** hide empty sections instead of showing "No items found." These pages are templated, so the unique part is what ranks.

## Comparison / explainer
**Job:** win "X vs Y" and "what is X" searches, and be the passage AI answers quote.
**Required:** a quick-answer paragraph up top (2–3 sentences) · definitions · a comparison table · when each option fits · a named author
**Minimum:** 1,000 words
**Notes:** be fair to the alternative. One-sided comparisons don't get cited.

## Original research
**Job:** earn links, citations and sales conversations.
**Required:** an HTML findings page (the gated PDF is extra, not a replacement) · methodology and sample size · each key statistic as a self-contained sentence · charts with text descriptions · a named author
**Minimum:** 1,200 words on the findings page
**Notes:** never publish the page with only a download form. The findings page is what ranks.

## Thought leadership
**Job:** show how the company thinks, under a named expert's byline.
**Required:** a clear claim in the first paragraph · a specific example or data point · what the reader should do differently · the author's bio
**Minimum:** 800 words
**Notes:** easiest from a 30-minute interview with the expert, drafted by marketing and approved by the expert.

## Press release
**Job:** announce, and give syndication partners something to link to.
**Required:** headline with the news · who, what, why it matters in the first paragraph · a quote · a link to the relevant product or story page · boilerplate
**Minimum:** 300 words
**Notes:** publish on your own site first and syndicate 3–7 days later, with a link back.

## In the news (coverage)
**Job:** credit third-party coverage without duplicating it.
**Required:** a 150–300 word summary in your words · outlet and date · a working link to the original
**Minimum:** 150 words
**Notes:** never republish the full article without written permission. If there's no summary, noindex it.
